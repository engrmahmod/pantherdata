Privacy Policy — Panther Data

Panther Data (pantherdata.com.ng) sells data, airtime, cable TV and electricity services in Nigeria. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the Panther Data app and website collect, why, and who it is shared with.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your full name, username, email address and phone number, as entered at sign-up.
  • Verification and security: email verification codes sent to you at sign-up, and your 4-digit transaction PIN, which is required before purchases. Passwords and PINs are stored in protected (hashed) form, not as plain text. Never share your PIN or verification code with anyone.
  • Transactions: your wallet balance and transaction history, and the details you enter for a purchase — for example a phone number for data or airtime, an IUC/smartcard number for cable TV, or a meter number for electricity — plus the amount and the service bought.
  • Wallet funding: when you fund your wallet, the funding account details shown to you (your permanent reserved account) and the amount paid, so the payment can be matched to your wallet.

We do not ask for your BVN or NIN. For your permanent reserved account, we use only your name and account reference.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email, and let you sign in.
  • To process your purchases, credit your wallet, handle refunds for failed purchases, and show your transaction history.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the service:

  • Payment processing provider — to provide your funding accounts and confirm payments into your wallet.
  • Service delivery provider — to deliver the data, airtime, cable TV or electricity purchase you requested, using the number or account details you entered.
  • Email delivery provider — to send sign-up and sign-in verification codes to your email address.

These providers receive only what is needed for their part of the service.

Security

Access to customer accounts, wallets and transactions requires sign-in, and purchases require your transaction PIN. Admin access is protected by its own sign-in and session controls. No system is completely immune to attack, so keep your password and PIN private and tell us at once if you believe your account has been accessed by someone else.

Your choices

  • You can update your account details in your profile.
  • To ask for your account or data to be deleted, email us at pantherdata1@gmail.com. We will delete your account and personal details within 7 days of confirming your request. Transaction history is kept for 1 year.

Contact

For privacy questions, email us at pantherdata1@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Continued use of Panther Data after a change means you accept the updated policy.
