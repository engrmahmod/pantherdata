Panther Data FAQ

Everything you need to know before you start buying cheap data, airtime and bills in Nigeria.

What is Panther Data?

Panther Data is a Nigerian platform for buying cheap mobile data, airtime, cable TV subscriptions and electricity bills — instantly, from one wallet, 24/7. Our promise is simple: low prices, fast service.

How fast is delivery?

Most data and airtime orders complete within seconds. Cable and electricity payments are processed immediately and confirmed by SMS.

How do I fund my wallet?

After signing up you get a permanent bank account number in your name. Transfer any amount to it and your wallet is credited automatically. You can also fund manually: transfer to our account details shown on the Fund Wallet page and your wallet is credited by hand.

Which networks and services are supported?

MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile for data and airtime — plus DStv, GOtv, Startimes and all major electricity distribution companies (Discos). See our prices for the popular plans.

What if my transaction fails?

Failed transactions are refunded to your wallet automatically, in full. You will see a clear message confirming the refund, and our support team is one WhatsApp message away.

Is there any signup fee?

No. Creating an account and getting your wallet is completely free.

Do I get a token for electricity payments?

For prepaid meters, yes — your token appears on your receipt and can be copied from there. Postpaid bills have no token, so no token is shown.

Is there a referral reward?

Yes. Share your referral link from your Profile. When 5 friends sign up and verify their email through your link, you earn ₦500 wallet credit — and again at every multiple of 5.

Is Panther Data an app?

Yes, it installs on your phone like a normal app, with the same account and wallet. On Android: open the site in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install. On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. No Play Store needed.

How do I contact support?

Tap the WhatsApp button on this page or on the website and our team will help you. For privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy.

Is my personal information safe?

Your account needs only your name, phone number and email. Funding accounts are issued with your name and a reference only — no BVN is required. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time; see our Privacy Policy for details.
